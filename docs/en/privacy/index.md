# Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-08-13

This policy applies to the Motara application and the invitation pages at www.motara.org.

## Data processed

Depending on the functions you use, Motara may process camera frames, facial or body tracking values, model and scene files, screenshots, a local installation identity, friend records, invitation tokens, device and network details for peer collaboration, and diagnostic logs. These data are processed locally unless you start a collaboration action.

## Network activity

Motara does not automatically upload tracking frames, models, scenes, or screenshots. Opening an invitation, joining a session, or transferring a model package can create peer or service network connections after you start the action.

The invitation pages do not send, store, decode, or log token values. They only construct a local motara:// link after you click the open button.

## Control and deletion

You can stop tracking and collaboration, remove local friends and scenes, and delete local application data through your operating system. Do not share invitation URLs publicly. A token in the URL may grant the corresponding invitation capability until it expires.

Motara does not sell personal information. Contact [hello@motara.org](mailto:hello@motara.org) for privacy questions.
